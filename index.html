Who I Am & What I Explore

I am an undergraduate Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at Kumaraguru College of Technology (KCT). My focus centers around understanding energy dynamics—from high-voltage industrial machines and utility solar installations down to low-power embedded microcontrollers and IoT telemetry.

Rather than treating engineering purely as abstract theory, I continually seek practical experimentation: building energy monitoring test benches, running sensor comparative analysis, participating in technical hackathons, and studying real-world manufacturing plants.
